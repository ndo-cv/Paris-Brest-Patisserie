Our Story

Where Every Morning
Feels Like Paris

Nestled in the heart of Southgate at 9 Hampden Square, Paris-Brest Patisserie is more than a café — it's a daily ritual. We open our doors at 7am every morning so that your day can begin the right way: with something truly beautiful.

We believe great food starts with great ingredients. Every pastry is handmade fresh each morning, every coffee is brewed with care, and every dish on our menu is crafted with the kind of attention that transforms a simple meal into a moment worth remembering.

Whether you're settling in with your laptop, meeting a friend for brunch, enjoying a leisurely lunch, or simply pausing the day for a coffee and something sweet — Paris-Brest is your place. Relaxed, welcoming, and always warm.

The Experience

More Than
a Café

Paris-Brest is a place where time slows down, flavours come alive, and every visit feels like a small escape.

🌅
Morning Ritual
We open at 7am, ready to start your day with a perfectly brewed coffee and something fresh from the oven. Begin every morning beautifully.
🥐
Artisan Breakfast
From classic full English to eggs Benedict and handmade pastries — our breakfast menu is crafted to fuel and delight you from the very first bite.
☀️
Brunch & Lunch
Whether it's a lazy weekend brunch or a working lunch, our menu transitions beautifully through the day with fresh, seasonal dishes to satisfy every appetite.
☕
Specialty Coffee
Our baristas take coffee seriously. From espresso to cold brew, every cup is crafted with skill, care and premium beans roasted for flavour, not volume.
💻
Work in Comfort
Free Wi-Fi, ample power points, a relaxed atmosphere and an endless supply of great coffee — Paris-Brest is Southgate's favourite remote workspace.
🌿
Outdoor Seating
On fine days, step outside and enjoy your coffee or brunch al fresco. Our outdoor seating area is a peaceful spot to unwind and watch the world go by.
