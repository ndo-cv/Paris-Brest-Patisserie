Our Story

Where Paris Meets
Your Neighbourhood

Paris-Brest Patisserie was founded on a simple, unwavering belief: that extraordinary food and coffee should be a daily pleasure, not an occasional indulgence. Nestled in the heart of Hampden Square, our patisserie has been a beloved cornerstone of the North London community for over seven years.

Every morning, before the first customer arrives, our kitchen is alive. Croissants are carefully laminated, pastries are pulled from the oven, and our baristas dial in the espresso to absolute perfection. This is the standard we set ourselves each and every day — because we believe you deserve nothing less.

We draw inspiration from the great Parisian café culture — the unhurried breakfasts, the beautifully presented plates, the conversation that flows as freely as the coffee. Our menu celebrates French tradition with modern British sensibility, using fresh, seasonal ingredients from trusted artisan suppliers.

Whether you arrive alone with a book, with a friend for a leisurely brunch, with your laptop for a working morning, or with your family on a Sunday — Paris-Brest is always your place. The door is always open. The coffee is always ready. And the welcome is always genuine.

— Crafted with love. Served with pride.
